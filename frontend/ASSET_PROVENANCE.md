# Stride Premium — Asset provenance

All three raster assets in `public/assets/` were generated for this project with the built-in ImageGen tool. They are conceptual editorial imagery and do not depict real Stride members, trainers, or endorsers.

- `stride-hero-editorial.jpg` — premium private training studio at dawn with a mature editorial model performing a controlled sled push; carbon stone and warm architectural light; wide composition; no logos or text.
- `stride-recovery-editorial.jpg` — composed late-50s editorial model performing a controlled mobility exercise in a warm private studio; portrait composition; no logos or medical claims.
- `stride-equipment-editorial.jpg` — brushed-black steel dumbbells, charcoal towel, and abstract unbranded training card on honed basalt; luxury still life; no readable text.

Icons are from the Solar icon family through Iconify. Fonts are locally bundled from Fontsource: Manrope Variable and Newsreader Variable.
