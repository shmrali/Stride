COMMON QUESTIONS

Helpful information before you join.

Do I need to be fit before joining Stride?

No. Stride welcomes every starting point. Your trainer can help you begin with a plan that suits your current fitness level.

Can Stride support me after an injury or surgery?

Stride can provide gradual fitness support after you have clearance from your doctor or physiotherapist. Trainers do not diagnose or treat injuries.

Can I speak with a trainer before I join?

Yes. The Stride team can help you understand which programme and level of support may suit your goals.

Does Stride provide medical advice?

No. Stride provides general fitness information. For medical questions, diagnosis or treatment decisions, please speak with an appropriate qualified healthcare professional.
